THE TWIST

Shorter isn’t automatically faster.

The short cable cannot connect two Ethernet ports as shown. It needs a correctly terminated plug on both ends.

Medium and long can deliver the same data rate. If both are properly made, compatible with the devices and within the relevant Ethernet specification, a longer cable does not automatically mean lower throughput.

A shorter cable has slightly less signal travel time. That is different from the negotiated link speed. Cable category, condition, length limits and the connected devices all matter.

Want one clear winner? Measure the cables and test their links first. Only call the medium one uniquely correct if the long cable has a verified limitation.

This is a prediction poll, not a speed test. The app cannot measure the physical cables.
