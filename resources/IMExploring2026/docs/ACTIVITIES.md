# Activity guide

All three activities use local in-browser MediaPipe results. AI Mirror uses Holistic; Cyber Shield and Music Painter use a lighter hand-only graph. Both graphs warm up once at startup, and switching activities does not restart the camera.

## 1. AI Mirror

- **Do:** step into frame, raise hands, pose, or make a fist.
- **See:** segmentation contour, pose skeleton, face contours, hand landmarks, and flowing particles.
- **Learn:** a literal silhouette and a landmark skeleton are different representations.

## 2. Cybersecurity Shield

- **Do:** move either hand into a falling message to block it. Let expected, safe messages continue.
- **See:** hand shields, safe and suspicious message examples, score, and immediate explanation.
- **Learn:** before clicking, check the sender, urgency, destination, request for credentials, and unexpected attachments. Color is a teaching aid in this game; real email clients cannot reliably label every message.

## 3. Gesture Music Painter

- **Do:** draw in the air with either index finger. Optionally turn sound on.
- **See:** persistent neon trails; vertical hand position selects notes.
- **Learn:** one coordinate stream can control both graphics and sound.
- **Privacy:** sound is generated by Web Audio. The activity does not request or use a microphone.

## Shared controls

- Activity buttons or keys `1`–`3`: switch activity.
- `R`: reset the current score or paint trails.
- `T`: change color theme in AI Mirror.
- `M`: toggle Music Painter sound.
- `F`: toggle full screen.
- **Reduced effects:** use 12 Hz tracking, fewer particles, shorter paint trails, and fewer simultaneous game messages.

Switches use a short fade, and each activity has an activity-specific no-person prompt. Camera permission and unavailable-camera recovery are shared by all activities.
